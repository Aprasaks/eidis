# Low-power wake and conversational session V1
- Keep explicit standby menu opt-in, existing DEKA classifier and owner voice thresholds. Quiet PCM below RMS 30 skips the neural wake inference after a two-second tail; 1.6 seconds of pre-roll are replayed at onset. This is an energy gate, not a true speech/non-speech classifier. The microphone and core video continue operating. Speaker model is still loaded per worker; no speaker inference unless a candidate is detected. ONNX uses one thread. No claim of Siri hardware-level power.
- Authenticated wake begins a hands-free multi-turn session, greeting immediately through existing local voice. Each question continues to require existing voice verification. After playback finishes and a 250ms acoustic tail, resume recognition without another wake word. Return to wake standby after 12s of empty capture, voice rejection, explicit end phrase, or session limit checked between turns (10 minutes/30 replies). Toggle off cancels active wake session. No simultaneous wake and STT microphone; no new audio files or transmission.
- Presence checks and end phrases are handled locally without Qwen. Actual standby wake token remains DEKA; JARVIS name is also accepted as a prefix once speech recognition is active. A compound wake phrase is acknowledged, not transcribed as a full command before greeting.
- Shorten ordinary Qwen request and startup preload keep_alive to 2m (research/other routes unchanged). Tradeoff: possible cold-load delay after longer idle. Core appearance unchanged.
- Offline synthetic benchmark: 60 seconds of digital silence, wake classifier 750 calls -> 0 calls, loop process CPU time 1.220459 -> .003386 seconds (not full app energy or elapsed mic standby). Three synthetic voices at three amplitude/noise settings, baseline and gated both detect 8/9; one common miss, no new miss in this small sample. Separate gated synthetic matched/nonowner wake smoke passes. 305 Python tests including source contracts, native local phrase tests, Swift build. Actual mic response, battery, false wake rate, conversational follow-ups and low-voice recall need live testing. No claim of 24h or actual 1–2% CPU.
